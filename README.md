# Why jigsaw pieces cannot slide apart

An online note, with interactive figures, on which shapes of the cut between two jigsaw pieces force one to lift a piece in order to separate them.

Read it at https://rotemassouline.github.io/puzzles/.

The note states Theorem 2 without proof. A full proof is archived in [proof-of-theorem-2.html](proof-of-theorem-2.html) (rendered at https://rotemassouline.github.io/puzzles/proof-of-theorem-2.html).
